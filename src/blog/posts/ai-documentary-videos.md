In this guide, you will learn how to create cinematic, walking-style AI documentary videos from start to finish. Using a structured documentary master prompt, you can transform a single story idea into a fully scripted, voiced, animated, and edited video without having to build every scene from scratch.

This paper-collage documentary style has become widely popular on platforms like YouTube and social media. Channels like Vox and Fern have proven that viewers love clear, visual storytelling built with textured cutouts, archival maps, newsprint scraps, and stop-motion motion effects that keep complex history or true crime mysteries engaging.

## What you'll make

* A complete documentary narration script tailored to your chosen duration and aspect ratio.
* A cohesive set of hand-cut paper collage images featuring halftone cutouts and archival textures.
* Short stop-motion style animated video clips generated from each scene image.
* A bold editorial thumbnail designed to capture clicks.
* A fully edited documentary video synchronized with voiceover narration and background music.

## What you need

* **ChatGPT**: Used for story research, documentary scripting, beat breakdowns, and generating structured image and animation prompts.
* **Google Flow**: Used with Agent Mode and Omni 1.1 Flash to batch generate scene images, organize assets, and animate clips.
* **ElevenLabs**: Used to generate realistic, calm documentary voiceover narration.
* **CapCut**: Used to assemble your timeline, synchronize video clips with the voiceover, and add background music.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1: Launch the Master Prompt in ChatGPT

Start by copying the master prompt displayed above this article and pasting it into ChatGPT as your very first message. Do not alter the instructions. The prompt acts as an interactive documentary engine that asks you questions one step at a time and calculates runtimes, word counts, and clip counts dynamically.

### Step 2: Choose your topic and video format

Once the engine begins, tell it whether you have a specific story in mind or want to explore a niche like unsolved mysteries, aviation, or dark history. In the tutorial, the mysterious disappearance of flight MH370 is used as the core example. After confirming your subject, choose your format: 16:9 landscape for YouTube documentaries, or 9:16 vertical for Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels.

### Step 3: Select runtime and generate the script

Select the length of your documentary. The prompt supports short projects under three minutes or longer multi-episode structures for five, ten, or twenty-minute stories. The engine writes a measured, cold-open script at approximately 150 words per minute. Review the narration to ensure it delivers clear facts without filler.

### Step 4: Generate voiceover directions and audio

Ask the prompt for voice directions, then head to ElevenLabs. The prompt suggests a calm, investigative narrator profile with stability around 55, similarity around 80, and speaker boost turned on. Paste your generated script into ElevenLabs, run two to three takes, and download the take where the opening sentence lands with the best pacing.

### Step 5: Produce visual beats and image prompts

Type the next command into ChatGPT to generate your beat breakdown. The engine splits your narration into distinct visual beats lasting between 4 and 6 seconds each. It then produces exact image prompts matching the paper collage aesthetic: aged newsprint backgrounds, halftone photo cutouts with rough edges, censor bars over real faces, and minimal accent colors.

### Step 6: Generate images in Google Flow Agent Mode

Copy the batch of image prompts and switch over to Google Flow. Using Agent Mode, you can queue multiple prompts at once rather than generating each picture manually. Google Flow automatically creates the images, and you can keep the assets organized and renamed in your folder based on their scene numbers.

### Step 7: Animate scenes with Omni 1.1 Flash

## Tips for better results

* **Respect the representation guidelines**: When dealing with real historical figures or tragedies, keep faces stylized with halftone cutouts or censor bars, and represent children through symbolic objects like an empty swing or small shoes.
* **Keep clips between 4 and 6 seconds**: AI video generation works best when movement is constrained to short bursts. Never exceed 6 seconds per clip to avoid visual distortion.
* **Batch your work in Agent Mode**: Using Google Flow Agent Mode saves significant time by generating, renaming, and managing assets in bulk instead of scene-by-scene.
* **Distinguish reconstructions from real records**: For real-world documentary topics like MH370, always verify your facts and remember that AI visuals serve as illustrative reconstructions rather than literal archival evidence.
